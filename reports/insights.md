# 📊 Key Insights

1. **Technology is the top-performing category** , generating over $800K in sales, significantly higher than Furniture and Office Supplies. Within Technology, mobile phones are the primary revenue driver, indicating strong customer demand.

2. **West region leads in sales**  with over $700K, while the South region performs the weakest. This suggests an opportunity to replicate successful strategies from the West in underperforming regions.

3. **Sales peak in November** (~$350K), driven mainly by Office Supplies and Furniture. This trend may be influenced by year-end corporate spending and seasonal demand.

4. **California is the most profitable state** (~$76K profit), with peak profitability occurring in December. Office Supplies contribute significantly to both sales and profit in this region.

5. **Consumer segment receives the highest discounts**  and also drives the highest sales volume, particularly in Office Supplies (11K+ units sold).

---

# 🚀 Recommendations

- Increase focus on **high-demand Technology products**, especially mobile phones, to maximize revenue.
- Apply **West region sales strategie**s to improve performance in the South.
- **Prepare inventory for November–December**, especially Office Supplies and Furniture, to meet peak demand.
- Analyze and replicate **California’s successful sales approach** in other states.
- Optimize **discount strategy for Consumer segment** to ensure profitability is not negatively impacted.
